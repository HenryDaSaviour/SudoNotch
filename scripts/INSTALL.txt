SudoNotch - a notch for your Mac (works with or without a real notch; needs macOS 14 or newer)

INSTALL
1. Drag SudoNotch onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open SudoNotch from your Applications folder.

SudoNotch is notarized by Apple, so it should open normally. If macOS still shows a
warning the first time, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

WHAT IT DOES
  - Hover the notch at the top of your screen to open it: music controls, clipboard
    history, a file shelf, AirDrop, and a Tools tab.
  - Tools: lock your keyboard so you can clean it, a switch that keeps your Mac
    running with the lid closed for a set time, and Caffeinate. The keyboard lock needs Accessibility access
    (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility). The lid switch asks for
    your Mac password once, the first time. Don't put a Mac in a bag while the lid switch is on.
  - Shows battery, Bluetooth and volume/brightness popups in the notch.
  - Rounds the corners of your display on Macs without a notch, with an animation
    when you use your macOS hot corners (System Settings > Desktop & Dock).
  - Starts when you log in. Change that in Settings (the gear in the notch).

If macOS asks to control Music/Spotify or use Bluetooth, click Allow. That's
what makes the music controls and Bluetooth popups work.
